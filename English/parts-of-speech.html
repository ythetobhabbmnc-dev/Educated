Parts of Speech

English Grammar Basics

1

Noun

A word that names a person, place, thing, or idea.

Example: Ravi went to the market to buy vegetables.
2

Pronoun

A word used in place of a noun.

Example: She gave him the book.
3

Verb

A word that shows an action or state of being.

Example: The children are playing in the park.
4

Adjective

A word that describes or modifies a noun.

Example: She wore a beautiful red dress.
5

Adverb

A word that modifies a verb, adjective, or another adverb — often tells how, when, or where.

Example: He runs quickly and finishes early.
6

Preposition

A word that shows the relationship between a noun/pronoun and other words in a sentence.

Example: The cat is sitting under the table.
7

Conjunction

A word that joins words, phrases, or clauses together.

Example: I wanted to go out, but it started raining.
8

Interjection

A word that expresses sudden feeling or emotion.

Example: Wow! That was an amazing goal.
